    Doodle Champion Island Games is a browser-based adventure game that combines exploration with multiple sports challenges. You play as a small calico cat named Lucky, who arrives on a mysterious island to compete against powerful champions and prove your skills.

    Gameplay

    The game blends sports mini-games with an open-world map. Instead of jumping straight into matches, you explore different areas of the island, meet characters, and unlock events as you progress. Your main goal is to defeat champions and collect sacred scrolls by winning competitions.

    How to Play Doodle Champion Island Games

    Doodle Champion Island Games is a mix of sports challenges and exploration. You control a character moving across an island, entering different areas to compete in events and earn medals.

    Start by exploring the island and interacting with characters to unlock mini-games. Each sport has its own rules and controls, so you need to follow on-screen instructions and adjust your timing for every challenge. Your main objective is to win events and collect all 7 medals by completing different sports.

    The game includes activities like archery, marathon, climbing, swimming, rugby, skateboarding, and table tennis. Each one tests your reaction speed and timing in a different way. As you progress, challenges become harder, requiring better control and focus.You can also choose a team and contribute points while playing, adding a competitive layer to the experience. The game uses retro-style visuals with animated cutscenes, making the journey feel more immersive while you continue competing and exploring.

    Controls

    Arrow Keys / WASD – Move
    Spacebar – Interact / Action
    Mouse / Tap – Select options (on mobile)

    Who Created Doodle Champion Island Games?

    Doodle Champion Island Games was developed by Google in collaboration with artists and developers including Colin Duffy, Nate Swinehart, Sophie Diao, Ben Tobias, Hélène Leroux, and Qumu Music.

    Tips to Win

    •  Learn each sport’s mechanics – Every event plays differently

    •  Explore fully – Side quests can help you progress faster

    • Focus on timing – Most challenges depend on precise inputs

    • Practice harder events – Difficulty increases as you advance

    Guest Artist Q&A – STUDIO4°C

    The animations and characters in Doodle Champion Island Games were created by STUDIO4°C, a Tokyo-based studio known for its distinctive visual style. Here’s a refined look at their insights behind the game’s design.

    • Character Creation Process

    The team started by exploring well-known Japanese folktales and stories featuring recognizable characters. They then matched these characters with the different sports in the game.

    Each champion was inspired by a specific story, while other roles—like team leaders and townspeople—were based on familiar cultural figures such as Kappa, Yatagarasu, and traditional lion dance characters. Designs stayed true to the original stories but were adjusted to fit the gameplay and visual style.

    • Favorite Elements of the Game

    One standout for the team is Lucky, the ninja cat protagonist, who blends a cool appearance with a cute personality.

    They also enjoyed building a world inspired by locations across Japan—from Hokkaido in the north to Okinawa in the south. This allowed them to create varied environments like oceans, tropical beaches, forests, and snowy mountains, reflecting the country’s diverse landscapes.

    • Favorite Characters and Sports

    A favorite character is the stone guardian (Komainu). Although traditionally seen as fierce, it was redesigned with a more charming personality and used as a guide within the game.

    For sports, table tennis stood out. Pairing it with the Tengu character—known for agility and movement—created a unique and fitting combination, even though it doesn’t appear in traditional folklore.

    • Message Behind the Game

    The team hopes players enjoy completing the game while also understanding its deeper idea: effort and persistence can bring happiness to others, even if that effort goes unnoticed.

    They also wanted to highlight a sense of reward and positivity through the journey, where small achievements contribute to something bigger.
